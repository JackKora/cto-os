# Product

**Scope:** The substance of the product response — strategy, product goals, the now/next/later roadmap, initiative lifecycle (discovery → validated → in-flight → shipped | killed), prioritization decisions, user-research findings, structured GTM/post-sale feedback (sales, CS, support, implementation, marketing, partners), the product-trio operating model (PM + design + engineering + data science), and the product function's operating cadence.

**Out of scope:** Company goals and customer signal at CTO altitude (Business Alignment); PM 1:1s, coaching, and team-wide comms (Managing Down); PM performance arcs, calibration, promotions, PIPs (Performance & Development); PM flow metrics like cycle time and WIP (Process Management, `flow_type: pm`); technical architecture / ADRs / tech-debt (Technical Strategy); org structure of the product function (Org Design); aggregated competitive positioning (Sales, not CTO OS — individual win-loss items land here as `product-feedback`).

**Frameworks:** [Richard Rumelt — *Good Strategy, Bad Strategy*](https://www.amazon.com/Good-Strategy-Bad-Difference-Matters/dp/0307886239), [Marty Cagan — *Inspired*](https://www.svpg.com/books/inspired-how-to-create-tech-products-customers-love/), [Teresa Torres — *Continuous Discovery Habits*](https://www.producttalk.org/continuous-discovery-habits/), [Janna Bastow — Now / Next / Later](https://www.producttalk.org/2020/04/the-now-next-later-roadmap/) + [Itamar Gilad — outcome roadmaps](https://itamargilad.com/the-evidence-guided-success-formula/). Module spine: Ravi Mehta's hierarchy (company mission → company strategy → product strategy → product goals → product roadmap → initiatives).

**Depends on:**
- Required: none (foundational on the product side)
- Optional: business-alignment, process-management, technical-strategy, managing-down, personal-os

**Example tasks:**
- "Activate the product module and walk me through current strategy, goals, and roadmap."
- "Sales got pushback on SOC 2 evidence from [enterprise customer] — log product feedback." (Edtech overlay: "Sales got pushback on FERPA evidence from [district].")
- "We're starting discovery on a new alerting flow. Add the initiative."
- "Re-anchor the roadmap — move [outcome] from next to now."
- "Prep for the product review with [PM]."
- "Where's product at? Show me roadmap, goal status, blockers in feedback."
- "Log the prioritization decision — we chose A over B because the value risk on A has stronger evidence."
