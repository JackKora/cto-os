# Negotiation

**Scope:** Consequential professional negotiations with partly shared and opposed interests, a meaningful agreement/concession/commitment at stake, a real alternative or walk-away choice, and enough complexity to warrant deliberate preparation. Owns strategy, sequencing, round planning and debriefs, multi-issue packages, closure, and negotiation-specific lessons.

**Out of scope:** Ordinary persuasion, feedback, disagreement, coaching, coalition building, relationship maintenance, legal analysis, routine purchasing, and personal-life negotiation. Relationship and subject modules remain canonical for their context; Negotiation reads that context only when relevant.

**Frameworks:** [Harvard Negotiation Project Seven Elements](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/what-is-negotiation/) always; [Chris Voss / Black Swan's *Never Split the Difference* conversational method and evolved concepts](https://www.blackswanltd.com/newsletter/expert-negotiator-concepts-that-have-evolved-since-never-split-the-difference-was-published) as the configurable default conversational layer; [3-D Negotiation](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/negotiating-in-three-dimensions), [Difficult Conversations](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/difficult-conversations-how-to-discuss-what-matters-most/), and [MESO](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/managing-the-negotiators-dilemma-nb/) only when their routing criteria fit.

**Depends on:**
- Required: none
- Optional: `personal-os`, `managing-up`, `managing-down`, `managing-sideways`, `external-network`, `business-alignment`, `hiring`, `budget`, `legal`, `product`, `tech-ops`, `security-compliance`, `org-design`

**Example tasks:**
- "Prepare me for the customer renewal negotiation and identify my BATNA and walk-away."
- "Plan round three; they moved on term but not scope."
- "Design three roughly equivalent packages across price, term, and service level."
- "We walked away — close the negotiation and capture the lesson."
- "Show every active negotiation and the next move."

**State location:** `cto-os-data/modules/negotiation/state/`

**Sensitivity:** high. Alternatives, reservation boundaries, leverage, authority, concessions, and counterpart hypotheses could be damaging if disclosed. The module never fabricates them and keeps sourced facts, user assertions, and hypotheses distinct.
