# Managing Sideways

**Scope:** Lateral relationships with peers — fellow leaders across functions (CPO, VP Sales, VP Marketing, Head of Ops, Legal, Finance, etc.) where the user has to get things done without reporting authority. Tracks observable peer profiles and currencies, 1:1 preparation and capture, coalition building, stalled asks, relationship work, and post-negotiation relationship signals.

**Out of scope:** Substantive negotiation strategy, BATNA and reservation boundaries, offer packages, sequencing, and round debriefs (Negotiation); direct reports (Managing Down); upward relationships (Managing Up); external peer CTOs and industry peers (External Network & Thought Leadership); board-level stakeholders (Board Comms); large-audience internal comms (Org Comms).

**Frameworks:** [Allan Cohen & David Bradford — *Influence Without Authority*](https://www.amazon.com/Influence-Without-Authority-Allan-Cohen/dp/0471463302) for ordinary influence and coalition building.

**Depends on:**
- Required: none
- Optional: `personal-os` (voice, show-up), `business-alignment` (shared company-level goals), `attention-operations` (surfaces peer-originated items), `negotiation` (linked post-negotiation relationship signals only)

**Example tasks:**
- "Prep for my 1:1 with the CPO tomorrow."
- "Log my 1:1 with the VP of Sales — what came out of it."
- "The VP of Marketing keeps deprioritizing the ask — help me reframe."
- "Build the coalition for the SOC 2 push across eng, sales, and legal."
- "Capture how trust with the CPO changed after the negotiation."
